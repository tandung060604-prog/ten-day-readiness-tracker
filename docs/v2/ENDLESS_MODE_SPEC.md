# ENDLESS COUPLE LIFE & SEASONAL EVENTS SPECIFICATION — LITTLE DAYS V2

## 1. Overview
Upon completing **Level 30 (Grand Sunset Finale)** of the adventure campaign, the app automatically transitions into **Endless Couple Life** mode. The game remains vibrant and rewarding forever through rotating daily/weekly quests, relationship milestones, and holiday events.

---

## 2. Endless Quest Generator Architecture

```text
Every Morning ➔ Fetch Date String (YYYY-MM-DD)
   │
   ├── 💧 Health Quest: Hydration & Fitness Tracking (+30 Hearts, +40 Coins)
   ├── 🛋️ Love Quest: Sofa Check-In & Daily Question (+45 Hearts, +50 Coins)
   └── ⭐ Adventure Quest: Replay Stage / High Score Sprint (+35 Hearts, +60 Coins)
```

---

## 3. Seasonal Event Schedules

1. **Valentine's Day (Feb 14)**:
   - Rose petal particle overlays, double heart gains from couple questions.
2. **Tết Holiday (Lunar New Year)**:
   - Red lantern festival themes in Town Square, lucky coin red envelopes.
3. **Christmas (Dec 24–25)**:
   - Cozy snow overlays on the Cottage, holiday music chime.
4. **Partner Birthdays & Anniversaries**:
   - Mascot cheer banners, party popper animations, and exclusive Memory Capsule unlocking.
