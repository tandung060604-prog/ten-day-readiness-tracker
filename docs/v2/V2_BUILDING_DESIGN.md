# LITTLE DAYS V2 — BUILDING DESIGN SPECIFICATION

## Overview
In Little Days V2, each building represents a distinct spatial and narrative environment rather than a standard SaaS dashboard. The interaction model adheres to:
`Scene Atmosphere → Focal Object Interaction → Secondary Activity → Companion Reaction → Economy & XP Reward`

---

## Canonical Building Design Profiles

### 1. Our Home (`home`)
- **Exterior**: Warm cottage with red brick chimney and flower boxes.
- **Interior Environment**: Warm wooden cottage living room, soft glowing fireplace, tea table, photo wall, window looking out into the town.
- **Focal Interaction**: Living Room Check-in & Memory Wall.
- **Secondary Interaction**: Love letter mailbox, bookshelf timeline, today's summary card.
- **Atmosphere**: Gentle wood crackle, warm daylight / evening amber lamp glow.
- **Companion Behavior**: Chiikawa sitting on the sofa reading; Usagi cheerfully organizing tea cups.

### 2. Quest Square (`quests`)
- **Exterior**: Town plaza with central notice board and celebration banners.
- **Interior Environment**: Sunlit cobblestone town square with wooden quest notice board, daily bulletin, and golden reward chest.
- **Focal Interaction**: Interactive Quest Board (Daily, Weekly, Couple, Story quests).
- **Secondary Interaction**: Daily streak chest claim, campaign level launch portal.
- **Atmosphere**: Cheerful town breeze, gentle bird song, lively chatter.
- **Companion Behavior**: Usagi bouncing next to the quest board, pointing with enthusiasm.

### 3. Training Dojo & Gym (`gym`)
- **Exterior**: Traditional wooden dojo with straw mats and training gear.
- **Interior Environment**: Polished timber training hall, dumbbell rack, soft tatami workout zone, rest bench, stopwatch.
- **Focal Interaction**: Workout Plan execution with completion checkmark ritual.
- **Secondary Interaction**: Rest timer with breathing audio, personal records board, exercise technique guide.
- **Atmosphere**: Energetic motivation beats, rhythmic breath cues.
- **Companion Behavior**: Usagi acting as an enthusiastic, high-energy fitness coach with victory dances.

### 4. Magic Water Fountain (`water`)
- **Exterior**: Grand marble fountain with crystalline water jets.
- **Interior Environment**: Shimmering stone courtyard centered on an animated magical fountain. Water levels visibly rise from 0% to 100% based on daily hydration logs.
- **Focal Interaction**: Water logging via Quick Pours (+200ml, +300ml, +500ml) with water level physics.
- **Secondary Interaction**: Hydration streak tracker, historical hydration journal behind a stone tablet.
- **Atmosphere**: Soothing bubbling water, crystal splashing chime, rainbow light caustics at 100%.
- **Companion Behavior**: Chiikawa happily splashing water droplets with cheerful vocalization.

### 5. Sleep Haven (`sleep`)
- **Exterior**: Crescent moon tower with glowing star lanterns.
- **Interior Environment**: Dim, cozy bedroom with a panoramic starry sky window, cloud-soft bed, night lamp, lavender diffuser.
- **Focal Interaction**: 4-7-8 Guided Breathing Circle for relaxation & Bedtime/Wake-up logger.
- **Secondary Interaction**: Sleep quality rating, gentle white noise/lullaby ambiance selector.
- **Atmosphere**: Calming ambient nocturnal hum, distant windchimes, lavender scent visual mist.
- **Companion Behavior**: Chiikawa curled up under a plush blanket, gently snoring with floating Zzz particles.

### 6. Memory Library (`journal`)
- **Exterior**: Grand storybook archive with stained glass windows.
- **Interior Environment**: Vaulted wooden library filled with towering bookshelves, velvet reading chairs, open journal podium.
- **Focal Interaction**: Storybook Memory browser (Browse by day/month with page-flip animations).
- **Secondary Interaction**: Daily gratitude note, time-locked memory capsule.
- **Atmosphere**: Quiet page turning sounds, antique clock ticking, soft dust motes in sunbeams.
- **Companion Behavior**: Chiikawa wearing small reading glasses, turning book pages carefully.

### 7. Photo Scrapbook Studio (`album`)
- **Exterior**: Creative photo atelier with vintage camera sign.
- **Interior Environment**: Art studio with a large corkboard covered in polaroid photos, scrapbooking desk, washi tape, tag stamps.
- **Focal Interaction**: Polaroid corkboard gallery with photo inspection & Memory of the Month spotlight.
- **Secondary Interaction**: Tag filtering, date captioning, romantic moment favorites.
- **Atmosphere**: Shutter click sound effects, warm studio spotlight, soft acoustic guitar.
- **Companion Behavior**: Usagi holding a polaroid camera, snapping photos with flash sparks.

### 8. Little Market (`market`)
- **Exterior**: Striped awning farmers market stall with fresh produce baskets.
- **Interior Environment**: Vibrant market stall filled with fresh fruits, bakery pastries, wooden shopping carts, and recipe boards.
- **Focal Interaction**: Interactive shopping basket grocery picker and meal planner.
- **Secondary Interaction**: Fridge inventory tracker, recipe discovery, nutrition balance summary.
- **Atmosphere**: Gentle market hum, wooden crate sliding, fresh bakery scent aura.
- **Companion Behavior**: Chiikawa balancing an apple on their head; Usagi rummaging through vegetable crates.

### 9. Date Night Restaurant (`restaurant`)
- **Exterior**: Romantic rooftop bistro with fairy lights and panoramic view.
- **Interior Environment**: Intimate candlelit dinner table set for two, glowing fairy light canopy, starry horizon backdrop.
- **Focal Interaction**: Romantic Date Idea Spinner & Interactive Wishlist.
- **Secondary Interaction**: Date night budget estimator, menu & outfit planner, post-date memory logger.
- **Atmosphere**: Soft romantic jazz, gentle glass clinking, flickering candle glow.
- **Companion Behavior**: Chiikawa and Usagi dressed as polite servers offering a sparkling drink.

### 10. Airport Terminal (`airport`)
- **Exterior**: Modern airport terminal with runway radar tower.
- **Interior Environment**: Sleek glass terminal lounge with flight departure board, rolling suitcase zone, passport desk.
- **Focal Interaction**: Interactive Departure Board & Trip Countdown.
- **Secondary Interaction**: Luggage packing checklist, essential travel document vault, boarding pass badge generator.
- **Atmosphere**: Ambient airport chime, distant jet engines, suitcase wheel roll SFX.
- **Companion Behavior**: Usagi sprinting across the terminal pulling a miniature wheeled suitcase.

### 11. Beach & Adventure Zone (`beach`)
- **Exterior**: Tropical coconut palm haven with ocean waves and surfboards.
- **Interior Environment**: Sunny coastal beach scene with ocean waves, sandcastles, sun umbrellas, and adventure map table.
- **Focal Interaction**: Trip Adventure Planner & Destination Milestone tracker (e.g. Nha Trang 10-Day Readiness).
- **Secondary Interaction**: Beach activity wishlists, packing readiness score, adventure souvenir collection.
- **Atmosphere**: Ocean wave surf, tropical breeze, seagull calls.
- **Companion Behavior**: Chiikawa wearing a straw hat; Usagi holding a surfboard and snorkeling goggles.

### 12. Town Hall (`settings`)
- **Exterior**: Stately clocktower manor with community noticeboard.
- **Interior Environment**: Elegant hall with antique wooden council desk, world map atlas, master control gears, archive vault.
- **Focal Interaction**: Profile personalization (Couple names, anniversaries, trip dates) and Privacy controls.
- **Secondary Interaction**: Audio/Graphics toggles, local backup/export/import, PIN security management.
- **Atmosphere**: Majestic grandfather clock ticking, brass gear turning resonance.
- **Companion Behavior**: Chiikawa sitting behind the council desk with a tiny mayor stamp.

### 13. Love Clinic / Wellness Cottage (`hospital`)
- **Exterior**: Victorian apothecary clinic with heart-shaped roof chimney.
- **Interior Environment**: Cozy herbal apothecary with tea kettle, botanical remedies, calming heat packs, cycle calendar.
- **Focal Interaction**: Menstrual cycle & symptom logger with boyfriend care advice engine.
- **Secondary Interaction**: Privacy disguise toggle (rename/restyle to Wellness Cottage), cycle notification preferences.
- **Atmosphere**: Herbal tea whistling, calming rain on windowpane, warm comforting ambiance.
- **Companion Behavior**: Chiikawa offering a warm cup of herbal tea and a heart hot-water bag.
