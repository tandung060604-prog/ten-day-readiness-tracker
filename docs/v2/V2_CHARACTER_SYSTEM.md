# LITTLE DAYS V2 — CHARACTER SYSTEM SPECIFICATION

## 1. Overview & Vision
In Little Days V2, **Chiikawa** and **Usagi** are not merely decorative stickers or static illustrations; they serve as dynamic, reactive companions who reflect the couple's daily journey, wellness milestones, and emotional bond.

---

## 2. Character Archetypes & Personalities

### Chiikawa (Haru's Companion Archetype)
- **Role**: Support / Memory / Heart Keeper
- **Personality**: Gentle, careful, deeply empathetic, supportive, memory-oriented, slightly timid yet courageous when helping their partner.
- **Color Identity**: Sakura Blossom Pink (`#ff8da1` / `#ffccd5`)
- **Key Gameplay Focus**: Recovery, sleep logging, emotional check-ins, memory preservation, stress relief.

#### Unique Abilities:
1. **Heart Shield**: Grants resilience against stress or creates a safety net for daily habit streaks.
2. **Memory Spark**: Highlights an unlogged memory or forgotten photo moment.
3. **Tiny Courage**: Grants a bonus retry or boost when attempting challenging fitness/wellness goals.
4. **Memory Magnet**: Organizes and surfaces cherished relationship milestones.
5. **Flower Bloom**: Sprouts celebratory floral visual effects and heart currency multipliers.
6. **Cozy Time**: Softens countdown timers and triggers calming ambient soundscapes.

---

### Usagi (Mai Trang's Companion Archetype)
- **Role**: Energy / Momentum / Chaos Power
- **Personality**: High-energy, bold, playful, unpredictable, fiercely encouraging, action-oriented.
- **Color Identity**: Golden Star Yellow (`#ffd166` / `#f59f00`)
- **Key Gameplay Focus**: Physical training, workouts, quick actions, packing sprints, energetic celebratory fanfare.

#### Unique Abilities:
1. **Ya-Haaa Burst**: High-velocity energy surge granting instant activity completions.
2. **Carrot Rocket**: Breaks through procrastination barriers and powers up daily checklists.
3. **Ura Rush**: Temporarily doubles coins and XP gained from streak activities.
4. **Bunny Jump**: Quickly teleports between map landmarks and highlights urgent tasks.
5. **Crazy Merge**: Instantly upgrades or prepares ingredients in the Little Market.
6. **Golden Carrot**: Wildcard power-up providing maximum bonus points on milestones.

---

## 3. Duo System & Love Link Meter

### The Love Link Gauge
- A shared synergy bar charged by couple interactions, simultaneous check-ins, workout completions, and love letters.
- **Capacity**: 100 Link Points.
- **Charge Triggers**:
  - Sofa Check-in together: +25 pts
  - Daily Hydration Target met: +20 pts
  - Workout completed: +30 pts
  - Love letter sent: +25 pts
  - Photo memory saved: +20 pts

### Little Days Miracle (Ultimate Duo Power)
When the Love Link Meter reaches 100%, the couple can trigger the **Little Days Miracle**:
- Triggers a synchronized screen celebration animation with Chiikawa & Usagi dancing together.
- Grants **+100 Hearts**, **+100 Coins**, and **+150 XP**.
- Unlocks a special temporary ambient rainbow over the World Map.

## 4. Couple Bond Progression System
Bond Level measures shared intimacy in the game experience.

| Bond Level | Title | Unlocked Duo Interaction | Special Perk |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Lv 1** | New Friends | Waving Hello 👋 | Base companion dialogue |
| **Lv 5** | Cozy Partners | High Five! ✋✨ | +5% Coin bonus on quests |
| **Lv 10** | Sweet Duo | Sit Together 🛋️☕ | Unlocks living room tea party scene |
| **Lv 20** | Inseparable | Hold Hands 🤝💖 | +10% Heart multiplier |
| **Lv 30** | Soulmates | Warm Hug 🤗🌸 | Unlocks custom golden character auras |

## 5. Audio & Voice Safety Guidelines
- All mascot sounds are synthesized using Web Audio API procedural chords (squeaks, brassy cheers, chimes) or original fan-made vocalizations.
- No copyrighted character voice lines or official anime voice rips are included in the repository.
