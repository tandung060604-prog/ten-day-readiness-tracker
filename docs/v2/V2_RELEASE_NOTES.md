## 🌸 Executive Summary
**Little Days V2** transforms the original Ten-Day Readiness Tracker into a heartwarming, interactive **Chiikawa-themed Town & Couple Adventure Game**. Designed for lovers to strengthen their bond, preserve memories, track wellness, and embark on a 30-level romantic adventure from their cozy cottage to Nha Trang's glowing sunset beaches.

## 🌟 Major Highlights by Phase (Phases 00–13)

### 1. 🗺️ World Map V2 & 13 Building Interiors (Phases 03 & 04)
- **13 Themed Buildings**: Cozy Cottage (Ngôi Nhà Nhỏ), Town Square (Quảng Trường), Memory Library (Thư Viện), Candlelight Restaurant (Nhà Hàng Ánh Nến), Wellness Clinic (Phòng Khám), Post Office (Hòm Thư), Beachfront Pier (Bến Tàu Nha Trang), and more.
- **Dynamic Daytime & Weather Engine**: Smooth transitions between Dawn, Day, Golden Sunset, and Night with glowing window lights.

### 2. 🎭 Chiikawa Character System & Web Audio Soundscapes (Phases 05 & 06)
- **Authentic Anime Character Models**: Chiikawa (💖 Empathy/Care), Usagi (⚡ High-energy/Humor), Hachiware (📘 Wisdom/Courage), Momonga (🧁 Pampered), Kurimanju (☕ Cozy Relax), and Rakko (⚔️ Master Protector).
- **14-State Mascot FSM Engine & 12 Abilities**: Dynamic reactions, affectionate interactions, and leveling bond perks.
- **Synthesized Vocal Synthesis & Subtitle Toasts**: Native Web Audio soundscapes, synthesized Chiikawa squeaks and Usagi "Yaha!" vocal cheers with floating subtitle accessibility toasts.

### 3. 🧩 30-Level Match-3 Campaign (Phases 07 & 08)
- **3 Epic Chapters**:
  - *Chapter 1: Ngôi Nhà Nhỏ (Levels 1–10)*: Introduction of match-3 mechanics, soft wooden crates, rocket boosts, and Level 10 Miracle Finale.
  - *Chapter 2: Xây Dựng Thị Trấn (Levels 11–20)*: Town expansion and landmark renovations culminating in the Level 20 Town Bloom Spectacle.
  - *Chapter 3: Chuyến Đi Nha Trang (Levels 21–30)*: Cam Ranh flight, Hon Mun coral diving, mud bath relaxation, seafood feast, and Level 30 Grand Sunset Finale.

### 4. 🪙 Virtual 4-Currency Economy & Building Upgrades (Phase 09)
- **Pure Virtual Economy**: Hearts (💖), Stars (⭐), Coins (🪙), and Travel Tickets (🎫) with **zero paywalls, ads, or real-money microtransactions**.
- **15+ Collectible Items & 3-Tier Upgrades**: Upgrade every town building from Tier 1 (Khởi Tạo) to Tier 3 (Hoàng Kim) to unlock exclusive perks.

### 5. 💌 Couple Life Features & Endless Mode (Phase 10)
- **Daily Couple Question**: 30+ curated questions with daily auto-rotation, answer archiving, and +15 Heart rewards.
- **Love Letter Mailbox & Time-Locked Memory Capsules**: Romantic letter writer and capsules unlockable on anniversaries or partner birthdays.
- **Date & Food Roulette**: Smart randomizer picking dinner ideas and dating activities based on mood and indoor/outdoor preferences.
- **Couple Bucket List**: Check-off dreams with confetti celebrations and memory card collectibles.
- **Endless Couple Life Mode**: Post-campaign infinite daily quest generator and holiday event engine (Valentine, Tết, Christmas).

### 6. 🔒 Offline-First Privacy & AES-GCM Encrypted Backups (Phase 11)
- **Zero-Tracking / 100% Local Storage**: All personal journals, photos, cycle data, and couple profiles stay strictly on the user's device.
- **Automated V1 ➔ V2 Migration**: Automatic detection and non-destructive schema migration with pre-migration safety rollback snapshots.
- **Web Crypto AES-GCM 256-Bit Encrypted Backup**: Password-protected exports and schema-validated imports.
- **PIN Privacy Vault**: 4-digit PIN lock for sensitive medical/journal views.

### 7. ⚡ Polish, Performance & WCAG 2.1 AA Accessibility (Phase 12)
- **Code-Split Bundling**: Sub-second load times with separate vendor chunks (`vendor-react`, `index.js`).
- **WCAG 2.1 AA Compliance**: 3px golden focus rings (`:focus-visible`), touch targets >= 44x44px, and full support for `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)`.
- **React Error Boundary**: Resilient crash-guard with Chiikawa recovery screen preventing blank-screen failures.
